Video messages at 120 fps.

Round videos record and play at your display’s refresh rate, up to 640 by 640. Photos and files go out in original quality when you ask for it, not squeezed by default.

Tabs and split view.

Keep the chats you live in open as tabs, and put two side by side when you are copying, comparing or just talking to two people at once. Tabs come back after a restart.

{{SHOT:Split}}

Everything is ⌘K away.

Jump to a chat, a message or a setting without the mouse. Type > for commands, @ for people and # to search messages.

{{SHOT:Palette}}

Read without being seen.

Ghost mode hides your online status and read receipts. Deleted messages stay on your Mac, and every edit keeps its history.

Each option is its own switch, and all of them start off.

{{SHOT:Privacy}}

Plugins that ask first.

Plugins are small TypeScript programs that run in a sandbox. Each one lists what it can touch, and you see that list before you turn it on. Transcription, translation and filters ship built in.

Free, and yours to read.

Mollygram is open source under GPL-3.0, built with Flutter on top of TDLib, Telegram’s own library. macOS comes first, Windows next, then Linux.
