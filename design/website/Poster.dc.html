Mollygram

The Telegram client for people who live in Telegram. Faster, more private, and built for a real desktop.

Download for macOS Star on GitHub

Free and open source. Windows and Linux are next.

Made for the hours you spend in it.

Circles at 120 fps

Video messages play at your display’s refresh rate, up to 640 by 640. Files go out in original quality.

Tabs and split view

Keep chats open as tabs and put two side by side. They are still there after a restart.

One shortcut, ⌘K

Chats, messages, settings and commands in one search. > for commands, @ for people, # for messages.

Ghost mode.

Read without sending receipts or showing up online. Deleted messages stay on your device, and every edit keeps its history. Each switch starts off.

Plugins that ask first.

Sandboxed TypeScript with a permission list you read before you switch it on. Transcription, translation and filters are built in. Write your own.

Free. Open. Yours.

GPL-3.0, built with Flutter on TDLib, Telegram’s own library. macOS first, then Windows, then Linux.
