Mollygram for macOS

A desktop Telegram client, made with care.

Mollygram is a free, open source Telegram client written in Flutter on top of TDLib, Telegram’s own library. It keeps everything you expect from Telegram and adds tabs, a command palette, private reading, plugins and video messages at 120 fps.

Download for macOS Build from source

macOS: first release · Windows: next · Linux: later · GPL-3.0

1. Video and files

Video messages that keep up with your screen.

Round videos are recorded and played back at the display’s refresh rate, so they stay smooth on 120 Hz and faster displays. Photos and files can be sent in their original quality, with EXIF stripped if you choose.

Playbackup to the display refresh rate, tested at 120 and 165 Hz
Circle size384, 512 or 640 px square
Lengthup to 60 s, the Telegram limit
CodecH.264 by default
Photos and filescompressed or original, per message
2. Tabs, split, palette

A window that works like the rest of your Mac.

Open chats as tabs, put two of them side by side, and reach anything from the keyboard. Open tabs are restored after a restart.

⌘KCommand palette: chats, messages, settings. Prefix > for commands, @ for people, # for messages
⌘TOpen the current chat in a new tab
⌘1 to ⌘9Switch tabs
⌘\Split view with the next tab
3. Privacy

Private reading, on your terms.

Each of these is a separate switch in Settings. All of them are off until you turn them on.

Ghost modeNo read receipts, no typing status, no online status
Keep deleted messagesMessages deleted by others stay on this device, marked as deleted
Edit historyEvery version of an edited message, with its time
4. Plugins

Plugins with a permission list.

Plugins are written in TypeScript and run in a QuickJS sandbox. A plugin declares what it needs in its manifest, and Mollygram shows that list before you enable it. Anything not declared is not reachable.

id: dev.mollygram.transcribe
runtime: js
permissions:
  media.read: [voice, round]
  storage: 1mb

Transcription, translation and chat filters ship as built-in plugins. Read the plugin guide.
